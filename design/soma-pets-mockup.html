Pet home
Steps

Pet home. Tap the house (or Let out) and the pet hops out and floats on the screen as it does now; it sits over every app until you put it back. Drag it onto the house or tap Call to put it home; inside, it naps (Zz at night). One pet, so every home tile shows the same one.

Steps. Today's count from the phone's step counter, reset at midnight. The pet walks on the grass while steps are coming in and stands still when you do; the bar fills toward your goal (10,000 for now, long-press to change) and turns blue past it. The wide size adds the week. Tapping it says how today compares, in the game's text box.
