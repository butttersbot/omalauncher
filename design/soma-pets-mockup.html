Steps

Pet home. The pet's room, as in the games: it naps on the bed (Zz), with the window showing day or night. Tap the room or LET OUT and it hops out to float on the screen as it does now; the bed is left empty. Drag it back onto the room or tap CALL to put it home. One pet, so every size shows the same one.

Steps. The data box is the widget's frame and the field is inside it. Today's count sits in the sky; the progress bar is the road he walks every day, along the bottom to a goal flag: the walked part is sunlit with his footprints, the rest still shaded and grown over, and he stands in the road as far along as you've got (68% of the goal, 68% of the way). He walks while steps come in and faces you when you stop. Past the goal the road turns gold (10,000 for now; long-press to change). The wide size adds the week, with the goal as a dashed line. Tap it for how today compares, in the game's text box.
