Try this

  • Drag any scrap. Tap ✎ in the dock, then draw, highlight, add a note or a sticker.
  • Tap “Dinner with Mina”: the card grows into a full screen and shrinks back.
  • Unfold: the cover page stays exactly as you left it and a second page opens beside it.
  • Ask Oma for a tracker: it lands on your page as a new scrap you can tap.

What carries over from Cove

  • The canvas is the water: content sits on it, controls float above it.
  • A theme is a token pack, so the same page re-dresses without moving anything.
  • Tapping a card changes its vessel, not its contents.
  • What the agent makes is ordinary page content you can move, draw over or throw away.

Not modeled

  • More than two pages, pinch-zoom and panning the canvas.
  • Resizing or deleting scraps, erasing part of a stroke.
  • Typed prompts, errors, and the lock screen and quick panel.
  • Real Cove kit art: stickers and tape here are stand-ins.
