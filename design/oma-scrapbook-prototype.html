Getting around

  • Swipe a page sideways, or use the arrow keys.
  • Pinch the page (two fingers, or a trackpad pinch) to zoom out to every page. Tapping the page name top-left does the same.
  • In that view, tap a page to dive in, or + for a new page.
  • Say where you want to go: “Open my trip page”.
  • Unfolded, you see the page you're on and the next one.

On a page

  • Drag any scrap. Tap ✎ to draw, highlight, add a note or a sticker.
  • Checklists, habit dots and the rice timer all work.
  • Tap “Dinner with Mina” and the card grows to fill the screen.
  • Icons are the real ones from your Fold; apps without one get the theme's placeholder.

Not modeled

  • Renaming, reordering or deleting pages; resizing scraps.
  • The wallpaper is generated only for City Pop so far.
  • Typed prompts, errors, lock screen and quick panel.
  • Cove's kit art: tape and stickers here are stand-ins.
