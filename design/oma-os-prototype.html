    Oma OS, as a phone you can poke

    Every surface is live: swipe inside the phone or use the buttons. Three looks share one structure, so you can judge the look and the behaviour separately.

    Look
    Go to
    Oma

    In the phone

    • Swipe up on home: apps, full height, no keyboard.
    • Swipe down: quick panel. Swipe up on the lock screen: unlock.
    • Tap the Oma pill: summon when idle, open the task while it works.
    • Approval arrives on its own, from the top, wherever you are.

    Not modeled

    • Typing a prompt instead of speaking, and image attachments.
    • Errors: no network, agent signed out, speech not understood.
    • Several tasks at once, and the task history.
    • The inner (unfolded) display and landscape.
    • Editing widgets, folders, and the Linux windows.
