Oma · now

Changed since the last pass

  • No clock on home; the status bar already has one.
  • No pill. Progress sits in the status bar, and approvals and results arrive as Samsung's own notifications.
  • The ring is thinner, with two light heads and a soft trail.
  • Cover and unfolded are separate layouts with different widgets.
  • Perimeter and Prose are out; five new looks are in.

In the device

  • Swipe up on home for apps; swipe down to close them.
  • Press the side button on the bezel to summon Oma.
  • Dismiss an approval with ⌃, then tap the status bar to bring it back.

Not modeled

  • Lock screen and quick panel: Samsung's, styled through Good Lock, so left out of this round.
  • Typed prompts, image attachments, errors, several tasks at once.
  • Editing and arranging widgets on either display.
